Our story

Built for Nashville.
Named for Dayla.

My name is Donna Spencer, and I am the proud owner of Dayla's Deli. A special name for me because it is named after my daughter.

We believe in giving back to our community and supporting causes that truly matter. We're redefining the classic deli experience with fresh, wholesome ingredients all crafted to fuel your day.

Located on historic Jefferson Street, we are more than just a place to grab a great meal. We are committed to building something unique and sustainable for our community.

What is a delicatessen?

del·i·ca·tes·sen

/ˌdelikəˈtes(ə)n/

"delicacies" or "ready-to-eat foods" — a shop selling fine, ready-prepared foods. We wear that word with pride.
